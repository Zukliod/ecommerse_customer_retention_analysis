Technical Architecture & Analytical Methodology

Core data engineering pipeline and strategic business findings

Download PDF Case Study (.md)
01 Data Pipeline & Schema

Ingests raw Kaggle relational tables (Olist Brazilian E-Commerce + E-Commerce Customer Churn), performing multi-table joins on customer_unique_id to construct clean relational dimensional models: customers.csv, orders.csv, and churn_logs.csv.

02 SQL & LOD Analytics

Advanced SQL queries utilizing window functions (DATEDIFF, NTILE(5), and DATE_TRUNC) calculate monthly acquisition cohorts (Month 0 through Month 12), RFM quintiles, and historical/predictive customer lifetime value.

03 Predictive Modeling

Supervised Random Forest Classifier achieves a 0.976 ROC-AUC, identifying Tenure (26.5%), Cashback/Discounts (9.7%), and Unresolved Complaints (7.3%) as the top three early warning drivers of customer churn.
